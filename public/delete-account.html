Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your EduQuinn account and personal data, and what that does and doesn't remove. It applies whether you use EduQuinn on the web at eduquinn.co.zw or through the EduQuinn Android app.

1. How to request deletion

EduQuinn does not yet have a self-service "delete my account" button inside the app, so a request currently goes through our support team:

Request account deletion

That opens our Support form with the topic and subject pre-filled — just confirm the email address on your account and submit. You can also email us directly; see the Support page for the current address. Send the request from the email address on your account where possible, so we can verify it's you.

2. What gets deleted

  • Your profile information: name, email address, password hash, and (for instructors) expertise/bio fields.
  • Your learning activity: course progress, quiz and assessment results, wishlist, and in-app messages you sent.
  • Any profile photo or coursework file you uploaded.
  • Your active login sessions, immediately.

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request.

3. What we may have to keep

Some records can't be deleted immediately because we're required to keep them for accounting, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes, or because deleting them would affect other people's records:

  • Completed order and payout records — kept for the period required by Zimbabwean tax and accounting law, with your name/email retained only as far as those records require.
  • Security and audit logs of account activity — kept for a limited period for fraud and abuse prevention, then purged on the normal schedule.
  • Messages you sent to another user, or a course review you left — the other party's copy of a conversation or an existing review is not deleted, but it will show as from a removed/deleted account rather than your name.
  • Certificates already issued for completed courses remain independently verifiable (see Verify certificate), since employers and institutions may rely on them — the certificate record is anonymised of everything except the credential itself unless you ask us to remove it entirely, which may affect your ability to prove you earned it.

4. Instructor accounts

If you're an instructor with unresolved earnings or an open payout, we will contact you to settle those first — deleting the account does not cancel money already owed to or by you. Courses you published are transferred to an "inactive instructor" state rather than deleted outright, so enrolled students keep access to content they already paid for; your personal profile information is still removed as described above.

5. A parent or guardian requesting deletion for a minor

If you're a parent or guardian requesting deletion of a learner's account on their behalf, use the same request form and note your relationship to the learner in the message — we may ask for reasonable verification before acting on the request.
