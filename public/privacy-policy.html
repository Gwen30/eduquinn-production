Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information EduQuinn collects from students, instructors, administrators and visitors, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the EduQuinn website at eduquinn.co.zw and to the EduQuinn Android app, which is the same service shown inside a native app shell. Instructors are also bound by the additional Instructor Terms & Privacy Policy, which this document does not replace.

1. Who we are

EduQuinn is an online course marketplace and live-classroom platform connecting students and instructors, including courses for primary, secondary, A-level, tertiary and professional learners, and a business/corporate training offering. References to "we", "us" or "EduQuinn" mean the operator of the platform at eduquinn.co.zw.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information you give us when you register: name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), role (student, instructor or admin), and, for instructors, areas of expertise.
  • Payment information needed to process an order: the amount, currency, coupon used, and payment method (card, mobile money/EcoCash/OneMoney via Paynow, or manual bank transfer). Card and mobile-money details are entered directly with our payment processor, Paynow (Zimbabwe); EduQuinn does not receive or store your full card number or mobile wallet PIN.
  • Learning activity: courses you view, enrol in or complete; lesson and quiz progress; certificates issued; reviews you leave; live class attendance; and messages you send through course messaging, live-class chat or support tickets.
  • Content you upload: course materials and media if you are an instructor, profile or assignment files if a chooser prompts you to pick a file or photo.
  • Device and log information: IP address, browser/app version, device type, and basic request logs, kept for security and troubleshooting (see "Security events" below).
  • Support and admin records: support tickets you raise, and, for administrators, internal notes attached to an account to help resolve issues — these notes are visible only to administrators, never to other students or instructors.

3. How we use this information

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To process orders, refunds, instructor payouts, and coupons.
  • To deliver course content, track progress, issue certificates, and run live classes.
  • To let students and instructors communicate about a course, and to respond to support requests.
  • To send account, order and course notifications you have not opted out of.
  • To detect fraud, abuse and security incidents, and to enforce our terms.
  • To improve the platform (for example, understanding which courses or features are used).

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

  • Paynow, our payment processor, to authorise and complete payments.
  • Instructors, who can see the name and enrolment/progress information of students enrolled in their own courses, and messages those students send them — never your payment details.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform (for example, hosting and, where configured, transactional email delivery), under obligations to protect your data and use it only to provide that service.
  • Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of EduQuinn, our users or the public.

5. Learners under 18

EduQuinn offers courses aimed at primary and secondary-school learners as well as adults. Where a learner is a minor, we expect a parent, guardian or school to have authorised the account and to supervise its use. We collect no more information from a learner account than is needed to deliver the course and process any related payment, and we do not knowingly use a minor's information for advertising. A parent or guardian who wants to review, correct or delete a minor's information can contact us as described in section 9.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie and browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The Android app stores the same session information inside its own app-private storage, not shared with other apps.

7. Data retention and security

We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet accounting, tax and dispute-resolution obligations. Passwords are hashed, payment card details are never stored on our servers, and administrative actions on an account are recorded in a security/audit log. No online service can guarantee perfect security, but we apply industry-standard practices (encrypted connections, access controls, and monitoring for suspicious activity) to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can review and update most of your account information from within EduQuinn. You may ask us to provide a copy of your personal information, correct it, or delete your account and associated personal data, subject to records we must keep for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution reasons (for example, completed order records). Contact us as below to make a request.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact EduQuinn support through the Support page, or email the address shown there.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and significant changes will be announced on the platform.

This policy is a plain-language summary provided for transparency and for app-store compliance purposes. It is not a substitute for legal advice; EduQuinn's operator should have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer familiar with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act and any other laws that apply to its users, before relying on it.
