Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains when a course purchase on EduQuinn is eligible for a refund, how to request one, and what happens to the instructor's share of a sale that is refunded. It applies to the EduQuinn website at eduquinn.co.zw and to the EduQuinn Android app. It works alongside our Privacy Policy and, for instructors, the Instructor Terms & Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

You can request a refund for a course within 14 days of purchase, as long as you have not completed more than 20% of that course yet. Requests inside this window are processed automatically once submitted from your Purchase History page. Outside this window, or past that completion threshold, we may still consider a refund at our discretion — contact Support and explain the circumstances.

2. Why there's a completion limit as well as a time limit

EduQuinn is a marketplace: when you buy a course, the instructor who made it is paid a share of what you paid (currently most of the price). The 14-day/20% rule is there so that a genuine case — bought the wrong course, it isn't what you expected, technical problems prevented you from using it — can always get a refund quickly, while discouraging someone from working through most of a course and then asking for their money back once they've already gotten the value out of it. If you believe your situation is a genuine exception to the 20% limit, Support can still review it manually.

3. How to request a refund

  1. Open Purchase History while signed in as a student.
  2. Find the order and select Request refund. If the order is still within the window above, this submits your request immediately for review.
  3. If the button isn't available, the order has either already passed the 14-day window or more than 20% of a course in it has been completed — contact Support if you still believe a refund is warranted.
  4. An administrator reviews every refund request before it is approved or rejected. We aim to respond within a few business days.

4. What happens once a refund is approved

Your access to the course is removed, and the payment is reversed through the same method it was made with wherever our payment processor, Paynow, supports that; where it doesn't, we arrange the refund by bank transfer or mobile money instead. The instructor's share of that sale is reversed along with it — refunded orders are never paid out to an instructor. This is also why an instructor's earnings on a new sale are held for a short period (currently a few days longer than this policy's 14-day window) before being eligible for payout: it gives any refund request time to be made and reviewed first, rather than EduQuinn having to recover money from an instructor's payout after the fact.

5. Free courses, coupons and bundles

A course with no charge (free, or fully covered by a coupon) has nothing to refund. Where a coupon or bundle discount applied to a paid order, an approved refund returns what was actually charged, not the course's full list price.

6. Live classes and certificates

Reserving or attending a live class tied to a course does not by itself affect refund eligibility, but it is taken into account as part of how much of a course you've used. A certificate can only be issued once a course reaches 100% completion, by which point an order would already be well past the 20% threshold above and outside automatic refund eligibility.

7. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge directly with your bank or mobile money provider instead of requesting a refund through EduQuinn first, your course access may be suspended while the dispute is investigated. Please contact Support first — it's almost always faster.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, including the 14-day window and 20% completion threshold, as the platform evolves. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and significant changes will be announced on the platform.
